These terms match the ones inside the app. Last updated November 8, 2026.

- ShotMatch is a demo built at HackUTD 2026. It can be offline or lose data, and comes with no warranty.
- Only add photos you took or are allowed to use. Credit the photographer when you share a result made from someone else's photo.
- Renders are AI interpretations of your photo. They are not records of a real moment, so do not present them as one.
- The 3D view uses Google Map Tiles under Google's terms. Map imagery is shown for planning only and never enters a render or an export.
- Do not use ShotMatch to follow or locate people who have not joined your shoot.
- The photos, models and data ShotMatch is built from keep their own licenses, listed on [Credits and licenses](/credits/). The Gaussian splat of the DUMBO spot is released under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- The text of this site may be quoted with a link back.
